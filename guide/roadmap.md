# PathFinder Roadmap — The Six-Phase Journey

PathFinder guides you through six phases. They are ordered, but they compound — work in a later phase feeds back into earlier ones (a coffee chat surfaces a STAR story; an interview rejection sharpens your direction).

| Phase | Domain | Core question | Highest-leverage idea |
|-------|--------|---------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | **Direction** | What am I aiming at? | A clear direction makes every later filter cheaper. |
| 2 | **CV** | Can I prove I fit? | Proof of work: show, don't tell. |
| 3 | **Jobs** | Where do I apply, and when? | Apply early — timing is a strategy. |
| 4 | **Networking** | How do I get a warm intro? | Referral leverage — the 6–10× multiplier. |
| 5 | **Interview** | How do I convert? | STARL — structure plus learnings. |
| 6 | **Tracker** | Is the system working? | Measure referral rate, not just volume. |

## Why networking is the deep slice

This documentation set builds **Phase 4 (Networking & Referrals) end-to-end** as the reference implementation, because it carries the central thesis: warm beats cold, and most roles are filled before they are ever posted.

The pattern proven here — source registry → structured knowledge → guide → learning path → decision rules → runtime integration — is the template every other phase follows. See [pathfinder_architecture.md](../pathfinder_architecture.md) for how the layers fit together.

## The Four Pillars diagnostic

If your search is not working, the [Four Pillars](networking/README.md#the-four-pillars-diagnostic) tell you *which leg is broken* rather than telling you to try harder:

- **No interviews from applications** → Pillar 1 (CV) or Pillar 2 (targeting).
- **Few applications convert** → Pillar 3 (networking — all cold, no referrals).
- **Interviews but no offers** → Pillar 4 (interview prep).

Start where your weakest pillar is. For most students starting cold, that is Pillar 3.
