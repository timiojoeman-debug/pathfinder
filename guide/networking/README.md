# Networking & Referrals

> The highest-leverage domain in the whole system. If you do one thing well, do this.

This is Phase 4 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md) and the deep vertical slice of the PathFinder Knowledge Model. Every guide here is backed by structured data in [`src/lib/knowledge/domains/networking.ts`](../../src/lib/knowledge/domains/networking.ts) and traces to the TechTalk masterclass sources.

## The thesis

Most job-search effort goes into the worst channel. Networking fixes that.

> 70–80% of roles are filled **before** a public posting appears. A referral converts **6–10× better** than a cold application.

So the goal of networking is not "meet people." It is to **manufacture warm connections that turn into referrals** into the hidden job market.

## The Four Pillars diagnostic

Networking is Pillar 3 of four. The model is diagnostic:

| Pillar | What it is |
|--------|-----------|
| 1. Personal brand | ATS-ready CV + LinkedIn + portfolio |
| 2. Targeted applications | Curated list, tailored, applied early |
| 3. **Networking & referrals** | Coffee chats + warm intros into the hidden market |
| 4. Interview prep | Technical + behavioural |

If your applications rarely convert, Pillar 3 is your broken leg.

## The frameworks you'll use

- **The Hiring Pyramid** — shows the four tiers hires come from (internal → manager's network → referrals → cold). You target the referral tier.
- **The Coffee Chat (four-part)** — open / their story / your story / forward motion.
- **Three Outreach Openers** — shared connection > content hook > curiosity & humility. Never fabricate the connection.

## The playbooks (executable)

- **Earn a Referral From Cold** — target list → find a peer → personalised opener → coffee chat → 24h + 30-day follow-up.
- **Sustainable Networking Cadence** — ~3 connection requests/week, 1 coffee chat per fortnight, weekly post, bi-weekly follow-up sweep.

## What to measure

- **Referral rate** — share of applications sent with a warm intro. The single best predictor of interview volume.
- **Active warm contacts** — aim for 10–15 real, logged contacts over a season.
