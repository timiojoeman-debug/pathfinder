# The Coffee Chat

> **Prerequisites:** [The Hidden Job Market](hidden-job-market.md), [Referral Leverage](referral-leverage.md)
> **Concept id:** `c-coffee-chat` · **Sources:** Coffee Chat Mastery Essentials; Wiki/concepts/coffee-chat.md

## What this is

A coffee chat is an informal **20–30 minute conversation** with a professional — usually over LinkedIn or video — whose purpose is to **learn and build rapport, not to ask for a job**. The US calls it an "informal interview." It is the primary tool for entering the [hidden job market](hidden-job-market.md) and the mechanism by which [referrals](referral-leverage.md) are actually earned.

## Why it matters

Referrals come from people who know you. The coffee chat is how a stranger becomes someone who knows you — and would vouch for you. Skip it, and you're left cold-asking for referrals (which fails) or cold-applying (which barely works). The chat sequences trust *before* the ask, which is exactly why it works.

## How it works — the four-part framework

1. **Open / rapport** — genuine interest plus one specific reason you reached out. Make it about them.
2. **Their story** — ask about their path and work; **listen more than you talk**.
3. **Your story (briefly)** — a crisp who-you-are and what you're building. Evidence over claims.
4. **Forward motion** — one light, specific ask: advice, an introduction, or "what would you do in my shoes?" **Never open with a referral request.**

The mindset shift: lead with a **confident, relationship-building stance**, not a job-requesting one. The two hardest moves (per the source poll) are *leading the conversation* and *asking for a referral* — the framework solves both by sequencing them.

## Common mistakes

- Opening with "can you refer me?" before any rapport exists.
- Talking more than you listen — making it about you, not them.
- **Skipping the 30-day follow-up** — the move 95% of people miss, and exactly where referrals form.

## Exercises

1. Draft a **personalised opener** to one alumni contact using the strongest honest variant (shared connection > content hook > curiosity).
2. Prepare your **two-sentence "your story"** — who you are + what you're building, with one piece of proof.
3. Run **one coffee chat this fortnight** and send a 24-hour thank-you that references one specific thing you'll act on.

## Reflection questions

- In your last networking message, did you lead with rapport or with a request?
- Whom did you have a good conversation with 30 days ago — and have you followed up with "I tried what you suggested"?

## Real examples

- **Good:** "You suggested I look at X — I tried it on my project and callbacks moved from 2 to 5. Thank you." (the 30-day follow-up)
- **Bad:** "Hi, I'd love to connect!" followed immediately by "Can you refer me to your team?"
