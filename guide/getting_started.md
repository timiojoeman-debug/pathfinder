# Getting Started with PathFinder

PathFinder is not a search engine for career advice. It is a teacher, a strategist, and a career operating system. Its job is to turn knowledge into understanding, and understanding into action.

This guide layer is the human-readable face of the [PathFinder Knowledge Model](../src/lib/knowledge/) — the same principles, frameworks, playbooks, and sources that drive PathFinder's AI mentor at runtime. Everything here traces back to the TechTalk masterclass material in the Obsidian vault (see [pathfinder_source_registry.json](../pathfinder_source_registry.json)).

## How PathFinder teaches

Every recommendation PathFinder makes follows the same contract:

| Layer | What it answers |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Action** | What should I do? |
| **Explanation** | Why does this work? |
| **Learning** | What concept am I being taught? |
| **Source** | Where did this idea come from? |
| **Reflection** | How do I adapt it to *my* situation? |

If you only ever read the action, PathFinder is a checklist. If you read all five, it is an education.

## The one thesis to internalise first

> Students who **start early** and manufacture **warm connections** outperform more qualified peers who start late and apply cold.

Two numbers drive the entire system:

- A **referral converts 6–10× better** than a cold application (referrals are 7% of applicants but 72% of interviews).
- Applying in the **first 48–72 hours** of a posting can **3–5× your callback rate**.

Almost every PathFinder recommendation is, underneath, one of these two truths applied to your specific situation.

## How to read a guide

Each concept guide has the same eight sections — *What this is*, *Why it matters*, *How it works*, *Common mistakes*, *Exercises*, *Reflection questions*, *Real examples*, *Next steps*. Assume you are intelligent but unfamiliar; the guides teach progressively, so read them in the order the *Prerequisites* lines suggest.
