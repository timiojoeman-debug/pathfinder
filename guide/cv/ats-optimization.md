# ATS & AI CV Screening

> **Prerequisites:** [Proof of Work](proof-of-work.md), [Quantified Impact](quantified-impact.md)
> **Concept id:** `c-ats-optimization` · **Sources:** Wiki/concepts/ats-optimization.md; 2026 CV Blueprint

## What this is

ATS optimisation is understanding **how a CV actually gets read in 2026** — and surviving each gate. There are three:

1. **AI / ATS filter** — software parses keywords, tools, location, and relevance to the job description, surfacing only the **top ~20–30%**. Fail it and you're auto-rejected in minutes–hours.
2. **Recruiter scan** — ~**6–8 seconds**, an "F-pattern" over the top half of page one.
3. **The black hole** — passed the AI but not picked: silence.

## Why it matters

Most CVs die at gate one, never seen by a human. You can have perfect [proof](proof-of-work.md) and [quantified bullets](quantified-impact.md), but if the CV doesn't clear the filter and the scan, none of it is read. This concept is about making sure your evidence actually reaches human eyes.

## How it works

- **Mirror the job description's keywords** — exact skills, tools, and role terms — without keyword-stuffing.
- **One page, ATS-friendly** — no tables, no columns, no graphics, standard headings.
- **Front-load impact** — put quantified achievements in the top half of page one, where the F-pattern looks.
- **Keep a master CV and tailor lightly per role** — don't rewrite from scratch, and don't ship generic AI phrasing (both filters penalise it).

Section order matters: **Role Title** (exact match) → **Impact Summary** → **Core Skills** (top-third keywords) → **Experience** (quantified) → **Education** → **Certifications**.

The strategic implication: because this channel is so brutal, the higher-yield route is often to **bypass it** via a [referral](../networking/referral-leverage.md), which can skip the ATS gate entirely.

## Common mistakes

- Tables, columns, and graphics that ATS parsers mangle.
- Keyword-stuffing, which recruiters spot and penalise.
- A role title that doesn't match the posting — an instant rejection.
- Burying achievements below the fold where the 6–8s scan never reaches.

## Exercises

1. Take one posting and **mirror its exact role title and top skills** into your CV's top third.
2. Strip any tables/columns/graphics; get it to **one ATS-friendly page**.
3. Confirm your strongest quantified bullets sit in the **top half of page one**.

## Reflection questions

- If an ATS only read your top third, would it find the posting's keywords?
- In a 6-second scan, what would a recruiter actually take away?

## Real examples

- **Good:** Role title matches the posting; core skills mirror it; two metrics per role; one clean page.
- **Bad:** A two-column graphical CV with a generic title and achievements on page two.
