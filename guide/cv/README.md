# CV — Proof, Screening & Tailoring

> The 2026 bar isn't "qualified" — it's **fit + evidence**. No proof, filtered out.

Phase 2 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md). Backed by [`src/lib/knowledge/domains/cv.ts`](../../src/lib/knowledge/domains/cv.ts) and wired into the mentor engine for `cv-analysis`, `ats-audit`, and `match-score`.

## The thesis

A CV's job is to make a recruiter's *yes* instant — by surviving two gates and then proving fit with evidence.

> An **AI/ATS filter** surfaces only the top ~20–30%; a **6–8 second recruiter scan** decides the rest. What passes is what shows **proof**, not claims.

## The concepts, in learning order

1. **[Proof of Work](proof-of-work.md)** — show, don't tell; the 2026 hiring bar. *(start here)*
2. **[Quantified Impact](quantified-impact.md)** — proof at the bullet level: verb + task + result.
3. **[ATS & AI Screening](ats-optimization.md)** — how a CV actually gets read, and how to pass each gate.

## The frameworks you'll use

- **CV Screening Funnel** — AI filter → 6–8s recruiter scan → the black hole.
- **CV Section Order** — Role Title → Impact Summary → Core Skills → Experience → Education → Certifications.
- **Proof at Every Layer** — CV, LinkedIn, portfolio, interview, AI fluency all carry receipts.

## The playbooks (executable)

- **Make Your CV Pass Both Gates** — role-title match, top-third keywords, ≥2 quantified bullets/role, one ATS-friendly page.
- **Build Proof With Thin Experience** — GitHub in the header, a metric on education, a Projects section with live demos.

## What to measure

- **Quantified-bullet ratio** — ≥2 numbered bullets per role.
- **Keyword match** — role title + core skills mirror the posting (no stuffing).

## How this pairs with the rest

Because the ATS channel is brutal, the highest-yield move is often to **skip the gate** via a [referral](../networking/referral-leverage.md). A strong CV plus a warm intro is the winning combination — proof gets you past the human, the referral gets you past the filter.
