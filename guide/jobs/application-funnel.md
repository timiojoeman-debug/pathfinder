# The Application Funnel

> **Prerequisites:** [Apply Early](apply-early.md), [AI Screening Reality](ai-screening.md)
> **Concept id:** `c-application-funnel` · **Sources:** Wiki/topics/application-strategy.md

## What this is

The Application Funnel is the **conversion math of a search** — from tailored applications through online assessments, screens, and finals to a realistic **1–4 offers**. A workable shape:

> ~**80–120** tailored applications → ~15–25% OAs → ~30–50% OA-to-screen → ~30–50% screen-to-final → ~30–60% offer on finals = **1–4 offers**.

## Why it matters

Without a funnel you judge your search by raw application count and feel either falsely safe or needlessly panicked. With one, you get a **target** (80–120 tailored roles, diversified across tiers) and, more importantly, a **diagnosis**: the funnel tells you *which stage leaks* so you fix the right thing instead of adding blind volume.

## How it works

Track every application with its stage: company / role / date / status / contacts / next action. Then read the conversions:

- **Few OAs from many applications** → top-of-funnel problem: targeting or CV (AI screening). Tailor more, or pick better-fit roles.
- **OAs but few screens** → assessment performance.
- **Screens but no finals/offers** → interview prep (Pillar 4).

Keep the top of the funnel wide by **diversifying** beyond one tier — big tech, quant, fintech, tier-2 — so a slow cycle in one area doesn't starve the whole funnel.

## Common mistakes

- Measuring effort by applications sent, not by stage conversion.
- Concentrating every application in one tier and starving the funnel.
- Adding more volume when the leak is actually at a later stage.

## Exercises

1. Build (or open) your tracker and **compute conversion at each stage** for your applications so far.
2. Name your **single weakest stage** and the one action that would fix it.
3. Set a cycle target: **80–120 tailored** roles across at least three tiers.

## Reflection questions

- If you doubled your applications tomorrow, would it help — or is your leak downstream of the top?
- Which tier are you over-concentrated in, and what's one adjacent tier to add?

## Real examples

- **Good:** 90 applications → 18 OAs → 4 finals; you spot screen-to-final as the leak and shift into interview prep.
- **Bad:** 200 applications, no tracking, no idea where it breaks, so you just send more.
