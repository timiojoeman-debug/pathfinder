# AI Screening Reality

> **Prerequisites:** [Apply Early](apply-early.md)
> **Concept id:** `c-ai-screening` · **Sources:** Wiki/topics/job-search-system.md; Hidden Job Market Tactics & Tips

## What this is

AI Screening Reality is the fact that **~85% of companies use automated screening**, so generic, untargeted applications are filtered out in minutes — which is why volume-only strategies fail in 2026.

## Why it matters

AI mass-applying inflated application volume to **5×+** what it was a few years ago. A single posting now draws **1,000–2,000** applications, ~80% irrelevant, and recruiters review only ~10–20%. Send 30+ untargeted applications and the expected result is **silence**. Believing "more applications = more chances" leads you to pour effort into the channel most hostile to it.

## How it works

The escape is not more volume — it's **tailoring** plus **warmth**:

- Tailor a strong **master CV per role** so it passes both the ATS filter and the 6–8 second recruiter scan.
- Reach the [hidden job market](../networking/hidden-job-market.md) via a **referral** to skip the AI pile entirely.

Use AI *correctly*: to tailor, to draft-then-humanise outreach, to generate practice questions — not to mass-apply or to rewrite the whole CV from scratch each time (generic AI phrasing gets penalised by both ATS and recruiters).

## Common mistakes

- One generic CV fired at 50 roles.
- Rewriting the entire CV from scratch per application instead of tailoring a master.
- Treating AI as a volume multiplier rather than a tailoring assistant.

## Exercises

1. Take your master CV and **tailor it to one specific role** — adjust the top third to match the posting's language.
2. Identify **one target** where a referral is realistic, and note who could give it.
3. Audit your last batch: how many were genuinely tailored vs. copy-paste?

## Reflection questions

- Of your recent applications, how many would survive a 6–8 second scan against the actual job description?
- Are you using AI to apply *more*, or to apply *better*?

## Real examples

- **Good:** 10 tailored applications to a target list, applied early, with referrals on the top targets.
- **Bad:** 30 generic applications, total silence, then 30 more of the same.
