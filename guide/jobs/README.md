# Jobs — Applications, Timing & Targeting

> The other half of the thesis: *start early*. Networking gets you warm; this domain gets you **first**.

Phase 3 of the [roadmap](../roadmap.md). Backed by [`src/lib/knowledge/domains/jobs.ts`](../../src/lib/knowledge/domains/jobs.ts) and the TechTalk application-strategy sources.

## The thesis

Two things win the application game, and neither is raw qualification:

> Applying in the **first 48–72 hours** of a posting can **3–5× your callback rate** — while **high-volume cold applying is now a losing strategy** against AI screening.

So the goal is to be **ready before postings open**, apply **day-one**, and **tailor** rather than mass-apply.

## The concepts, in learning order

1. **[Apply Early](apply-early.md)** — timing is a strategy; rolling admissions reward day-one. *(start here)*
2. **[AI Screening Reality](ai-screening.md)** — why generic volume fails, and the escape.
3. **[The Application Funnel](application-funnel.md)** — the conversion math and how to read your leaks.

## The frameworks you'll use

- **HIRE** — Direction → Intensify → Reach → Excel: the campaign-level strategy.
- **Posting Calendar** — watch early, peak rush in September, short windows (Google's ~2–4 weeks) in October.
- **Application Funnel** — 80–120 tailored roles → ~1–4 offers; find your leaking stage.

## The playbooks (executable)

- **Be Ready Before Postings Open** — master CV + variants + autofill + tracker → apply within ~2 hours.
- **Build the Monitoring Stack** — aggregators + company pages + alerts so you see openings day-one.

## What to measure

- **Time-to-apply** — hours from posting live to your application. The most controllable lever in the funnel.
- **Funnel conversion** — stage-by-stage, so you fix the leak instead of adding blind volume.

## How this pairs with networking

Apply-early + a warm referral is the strongest possible position. The networking domain's `dr-deadline-warm` rule and this domain's `dr-window-closing` rule are two sides of the same moment: *a target window is opening — be both early and warm.*
