## Guide template

Every concept guide follows the same eight sections, so learners always know where to look:

1. **What this is** — plain definition.
2. **Why it matters** — the stakes and the behaviour change.
3. **How it works** — the mechanism and steps.
4. **Common mistakes** — anti-patterns to avoid.
5. **Exercises** — do-it-now tasks.
6. **Reflection questions** — adapt it to your situation.
7. **Real examples** — good vs. bad, concrete.
8. **Next steps** — where to go next.
