# PathFinder Learning Paths

Curriculum journeys that turn TechTalk knowledge into staged practice. Each path has an outcome, ordered modules, completion criteria, and one do-it-now exercise per module. The structured source is `getLearningPaths(domain)` in the knowledge model.

## How to choose a path

| If you... | Start at |
|-----------|----------|
| have never networked, no contacts | Beginner |
| have contacts but few/no conversations | Intermediate |
| run chats already, want consistent referrals | Advanced |

Move up only when you meet the previous level's completion criteria — the paths are cumulative.

## Networking & Referrals

### Beginner — Build Your Networking Foundation
**Outcome:** you understand why warm beats cold and have your first real contacts.

1. **Why warm beats cold** — internalise referral leverage + the hidden job market.
   - *Concepts:* `c-referral-leverage`, `c-hidden-job-market`
   - *Done when:* you can state the ~6–10× referral advantage and explain why cold applying loses.
   - *Exercise:* write, in two sentences, why you'll spend the next hour on a warm intro instead of 10 more applications.
2. **Build your target surface** — the list and follows that give you access.
   - *Concepts:* `c-hidden-job-market` · *Playbook:* `pb-earn-referral`
   - *Done when:* 20–30 followed employers + 5 alumni identified.
   - *Exercise:* draft your 20–30 employer list and find 5 alumni in your target role.

### Intermediate — Execute the Coffee Chat Engine
**Outcome:** you run coffee chats on a sustainable cadence and follow up where others quit.

1. **Run a coffee chat** — apply the four-part framework without asking for a job.
   - *Concept:* `c-coffee-chat` · *Playbook:* `pb-earn-referral`
   - *Done when:* two coffee chats completed with 24-hour follow-ups sent.
   - *Exercise:* run one coffee chat this fortnight and send the 24-hour thank-you.
2. **Make it compound** — sustain a weekly cadence and the 30-day follow-up.
   - *Concepts:* `c-coffee-chat`, `c-referral-leverage` · *Playbook:* `pb-weekly-cadence`
   - *Done when:* a four-week streak of the cadence with a follow-up sweep done.
   - *Exercise:* schedule the weekly cadence and a recurring 30-day follow-up reminder.

### Advanced — Manufacture Referrals at Scale
**Outcome:** you consistently convert relationships into referrals into target roles.

1. **Convert rapport into referrals** — time the ask, pair with apply-early.
   - *Concepts:* `c-referral-leverage`, `c-hidden-job-market` · *Playbook:* `pb-earn-referral`
   - *Done when:* referral rate trending up; at least one referral secured for a target role.
   - *Exercise:* identify your three warmest contacts and the role each could realistically refer you to.

## Jobs — Applications, Timing & Targeting

### Beginner — Get Ready to Apply
**Outcome:** you understand why timing wins and you're ready to apply day-one.

1. **Why timing wins** — internalise apply-early and why cold volume fails.
   - *Concepts:* `c-apply-early`, `c-ai-screening`
   - *Done when:* you can explain the 3–5× early-application advantage and why generic volume gets filtered.
   - *Exercise:* find one target employer's typical opening month and note it in your tracker.
2. **Build your application-day kit** — be able to apply within ~2 hours of a posting opening.
   - *Concept:* `c-apply-early` · *Playbooks:* `pb-application-day`, `pb-monitoring-stack`
   - *Done when:* a master CV + one variant + autofill + tracker + monitoring stack exist.
   - *Exercise:* assemble your master CV, set up autofill, and star the relevant GitHub aggregator.

### Intermediate — Run the Application Engine
**Outcome:** you apply early and consistently across a curated target list.

1. **Work the posting calendar** — apply day-one across a cycle without missing short windows.
   - *Concept:* `c-apply-early` · *Playbook:* `pb-application-day`
   - *Done when:* you've applied day-one to at least one short-window target.
   - *Exercise:* map your top 10 targets to opening months and set alerts for each.
2. **Tailor, don't mass-apply** — use AI to tailor a master CV per role.
   - *Concept:* `c-ai-screening`
   - *Done when:* three tailored applications submitted from one master CV.
   - *Exercise:* tailor your master CV to three specific roles and log them.

### Advanced — Optimise the Funnel
**Outcome:** you read your funnel and fix the leaking stage instead of adding blind volume.

1. **Diagnose and fix the funnel** — use conversion to find your weakest stage.
   - *Concept:* `c-application-funnel`
   - *Done when:* you can name your weakest funnel stage and the action to fix it.
   - *Exercise:* compute your stage conversions and write the one fix for the weakest.
